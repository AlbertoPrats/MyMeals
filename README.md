# MyMeals

**Plan your meals for the week, cook in batches, and always know what's left in the fridge.**

MyMeals is a free meal-planning and meal-prep app for your phone. It comes with 37 healthy, budget-friendly recipes. It keeps track of the extra portions you cook, so leftovers get eaten instead of forgotten. It also turns a few days of meals into one combined shopping list.

**Open the app: [albertoprats.github.io/MyMeals](https://albertoprats.github.io/MyMeals/)**

- Free: no account, no ads, no subscriptions.
- Works offline once installed.
- Your plan and recipes are stored only on your phone.
- The app is in Spanish.

> Want help from an AI assistant to create, adapt and suggest recipes? Try **[MyMeals+](https://github.com/AlbertoPrats/MyMealsPlus)**, the same app with a free recipe assistant. The two apps are independent, so you can use either or both.

## What you can do

- **Plan the week:** a calendar strip at the top, with each day's meals (lunch, dinner…) below.
- **Use what you cook:** cook a 4-serving chili and the app keeps the 3 extra portions as leftovers. It offers them first next time you plan a meal.
- **Avoid waste:** leftovers show how long they keep in the fridge, you're warned before they go off, and freezing removes the expiry.
- **Meal-prep a few days at once:** pick any number of days, fill them yourself or let the app do it, and see what to cook and when.
- **Shop with one list:** ingredients from everything you'll cook, added up (2 onions, 600 g of rice), with tick boxes and the Swedish product names from the recipes.
- **Eat balanced:** each food group (legumes, fish, chicken, red meat, eggs) is compared with a recommended number of meals per week.
- **Keep your own recipe book:** scale any recipe to the servings you need, write your own recipes, and mark favourites.

## Install it

MyMeals is a web app you add to your Home Screen. It then opens full screen like any other app and works without internet. You don't need the App Store, an account or payment.

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open **[albertoprats.github.io/MyMeals](https://albertoprats.github.io/MyMeals/)** in **Safari**.
2. Wait a few seconds until the week appears, so the app can save itself for offline use.
3. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open MyMeals from the new icon on your Home Screen. From now on it works offline.

### Android

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

### Computer

It also works in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox). In Chrome or Edge you can install it from the install icon in the address bar.

### Updates

When a new version is available, the app shows **"Nueva versión disponible"**. Tap **Actualizar**; your data is kept.

## Getting started

1. **Plan your first meal:** on **Semana**, tap **+** next to *Comida* (lunch) or *Cena* (dinner) and choose a recipe.
2. **Repeat for the week:** when you choose a recipe with several servings, the extra portions appear at the top of the list for your next meals.
3. **Or plan a few days at once:** go to **Preparación ▸ Nueva**, choose the days, and tap **Autocompletar**.
4. **Go shopping:** in your prep period, open **Compra** and tick items off as you shop.
5. **Back up now and then:** **Ajustes ▸ Guardar copia** (see [Your data](#your-data)).

## How it works

### Semana: your week

The main screen shows a strip with the seven days of the week. Tap a day to see its meals. Use the arrows to change week and **Hoy** to come back to today. A dot under a day means it has meals planned, and the day's calories are shown next to its title.

Each day has **slots**, *Comida* and *Cena* by default. You can add others, such as *Desayuno*, in Ajustes.

**Adding a meal.** Tap **+** on a slot. You can:

- **Elegir de la lista** (choose from the list):
  - **Search:** type a name or tap a food group to filter the recipes.
  - **Leftovers first:** any portions still available appear at the top under *Raciones sobrantes*.
  - **Personas:** how many people will eat that meal.
  - **Cantidad:** cook the recipe ×1, ×2 or ×3, for example to have more portions for later.
- **Crear nueva** (create new): give the meal a name and, optionally, a group. It's saved as *por completar* (to complete), and you can write its recipe later in Recetas.

**Tapping a planned meal** shows its details. It says when it's cooked, how many portions the batch has and how many are left, and until when it keeps. You can:

- change the number of people;
- cook a larger quantity (×2, ×3);
- mark the batch as frozen;
- mark the meal as eaten;
- move it to another day or slot;
- open its recipe;
- remove it.

### Leftovers and the floating bubble

Every time you choose a recipe, the app records a **batch**, meaning one cooking of that recipe. For example, the chili makes 4 portions: the meal where you cook it uses 1, and the other 3 become **leftovers**.

- **Leftovers come first:** when you tap **+** on a later meal, they appear first in the list. Choosing one uses up a portion instead of cooking again.
- **The floating bubble** (bottom right) shows how many leftover portions you have. Tap it to see them, freeze them or discard a portion. It pulses when something must be eaten today or tomorrow.
- **Expiry:** each recipe says how many days it keeps in the fridge, so leftovers past that point stop being offered. If you plan one too late, the meal shows a warning.
- **Freezing:** a frozen batch no longer expires. This is handy for dishes marked *se congela bien* (freezes well), like lasagna.
- **Sensible limits:** the app won't let you plan more portions than you cooked, or eat a dish before the meal where it's cooked.

**Removing meals:**
- Removing the meal where a dish is cooked moves the cooking to the next meal that uses it.
- Removing the last meal of a batch removes the batch.

### Preparación: meal prep for a few days

A **prep period** is any number of days you plan together, for example Monday to Thursday. Create one with **Nueva**:

- **Primer día:** the first day of the period.
- **Número de días:** from 1 to 14.
- **Cocino todo en un día concreto** (optional): a cooking day, such as the Sunday before. Leftover expiry is then counted from that day.

Each period has three tabs:

- **Plan:** the days of the period with their meals; use **+** on any slot as usual.
  - **Autocompletar** fills the empty slots. It uses leftovers first, then picks recipes that balance the food groups, preferring dishes with several servings so you cook less often. You can choose which meals to fill, limit it to some groups, and set how many people eat. If the period has a cooking day, it can cook everything that day, but only dishes that keep long enough.
  - **Equilibrio** shows how many meals of each food group you've planned against the recommendation for those days. For example, 3–4 legume meals a week becomes about 2 for 4 days.
- **Cocinar:** everything you need to cook, grouped by day, with the number of portions and the cooking time.
- **Compra:** the shopping list for the whole period.
  - **Each dish counts once:** ingredients come from each cooking, not each meal, so leftovers never add ingredients twice.
  - **Added up:** amounts from different recipes are combined and converted (grams to kilos), with the Swedish product name where the recipe has one.
  - **Tick boxes:** checked items move to the bottom. So do ingredients you always have at home (salt, water…) and "to taste" items.
  - **Compartir** sends the list as text, for example to WhatsApp or Notes.

### Recetas: the recipe book

- **The list:** search by name or filter by food group. Favourites (★) come first, and recipes still *por completar* are listed at the top.
- **A recipe's page:**
  - its group, time, how long it keeps, and the equipment needed;
  - the ingredients, scaled to any number of servings with the **– / +** buttons;
  - the step-by-step instructions;
  - calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and fibre per serving;
  - buttons to mark it as a favourite (★), edit it, or delete it.
- **Writing a recipe:** tap **Nueva**, or edit an existing one. Write one ingredient per line, for example `200 g de pasta`, `1 cebolla` or `2 cda de aceite de oliva`. The app reads the quantity, unit and name by itself, so they can be scaled and added to shopping lists.

### Ajustes: settings and backups

- **Comidas del día:** turn slots on or off, add new ones (*Desayuno*, *Merienda*…) and reorder them.
- **Siempre en casa:** ingredients you always have. They go to the bottom of shopping lists.
- **Copia de seguridad:**
  - **Guardar copia** saves all your data to one file, for example in Files or iCloud Drive.
  - **Restaurar copia** loads a saved file, including on another phone.
- **Recetas incluidas:** reloads the included recipe collection. Recipes you've edited are kept.
- **Borrar todo:** starts from scratch.

## The included recipes

The app comes with **37 recipes** in five food groups. The recommended number of meals per week for each group is in brackets:

| Group | Per week | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Legumbres** (legumes) | 3–4 | Chili con carne, red lentil dal, hummus with pita, black bean burritos |
| **Pescado** (fish) | 2–3, one oily | Air-fryer salmon, poke bowl, fish tacos, prawn fried rice |
| **Pollo** (chicken) | 2–3 | Coconut chicken curry, fajitas, teriyaki chicken, shawarma |
| **Carne roja / cerdo** (red meat / pork) | 1–2 | Lasagne, bolognese with hidden vegetables, meatballs, Korean beef bowl |
| **Huevos** (eggs) | 2–3 | Spanish omelette, shakshuka, carbonara, vegetable frittata |

Every recipe lists:
- servings, time and equipment;
- how long it keeps in the fridge and whether it freezes well;
- ingredients, with Swedish product names (Lidl, Willys, ICA, Coop);
- step-by-step instructions;
- approximate nutrition per serving.

## Your data

- **Stored only on your phone.** There's no account and no server, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Works offline:** once installed, everything works without internet.
- **Deleting the app's icon also deletes its data**, so save a backup from time to time with **Ajustes ▸ Guardar copia**. The same file moves your data to another phone, or into MyMeals+.
- **Each person who installs the app has their own separate data.**

## Questions and answers

**Does it cost anything?**
No. It's free, with no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.

**Why isn't it in the App Store?**
It's a web app installed from the browser. That's why it's free, works on any phone, and can be shared with a link.

**I can't find "Add to Home Screen".**
On iPhone, the app must be opened in **Safari**. Tap Share and scroll down the list of actions.

**What happens to leftovers I didn't plan?**
They stay in the floating bubble until they expire, you freeze them, or you discard them.

**Can I change the recipes?**
Yes. Edit any recipe, or write your own, in **Recetas**.

**Can I use it on two phones?**
Each phone has its own data. To move it, use **Guardar copia** on one and **Restaurar copia** on the other.

**Is there an English version?**
For now the app is in Spanish.

## For developers

The app is plain JavaScript with [Preact](https://preactjs.com/) and [htm](https://github.com/developit/htm), vendored in `app/vendor/`, with no build step. It's a Progressive Web App (PWA): a service worker caches it for offline use, and data is stored in the browser's IndexedDB.

```
recetas.md            the recipe collection (source)
app/                  the app, served as-is
  data/recipes.json   generated from recetas.md
  js/core/            logic: recipe parser, planner, shopping list, autofill
  js/ui/              screens
tests/                logic tests (node --test) and browser tests (Playwright)
tools/                recipe builder, icons, local server
```

You need Node 22:

```bash
npm install
npm run serve            # http://localhost:8080
npm test                 # logic tests
npm run test:e2e         # browser tests at iPhone size (needs: npx playwright install chromium webkit)
npm run build:recipes    # rebuild app/data/recipes.json after editing recetas.md
```
